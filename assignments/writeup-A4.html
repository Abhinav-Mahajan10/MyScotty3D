A4T2b — Inverse Kinematics (`gradient_in_current_pose` and `solve_ik`) *`gradient_in_current_pose`*: For each enabled IK handle (bone $i$, target $h$): 1. Compute the tip position $p_i = P_i \times e_i$ in skeleton-local space. 2. Walk the ancestor chain from bone $i$ up to the root. 3. For each ancestor bone $b$, compute the base-of-rotation point $r = M_{\text{joint}} \times \mathbf{0}$ (where $M_{\text{joint}}$ is the transform up to but not including $b$'s own rotations). 4. Compute the three rotation-axis directions in skeleton space using the geometric cross-product formula: - $\partial/\partial rx_b$: axis $= (M_{\text{joint}} R_z R_y R_x).\text{rotate}(\hat{x})$, derivative $= \text{axis} \times (p_i - r)$. - $\partial/\partial ry_b$: axis $= (M_{\text{joint}} R_z R_y).\text{rotate}(\hat{y})$, derivative $= \text{axis} \times (p_i - r)$. - $\partial/\partial rz_b$: axis $= (M_{\text{joint}} R_z).\text{rotate}(\hat{z})$, derivative $= \text{axis} \times (p_i - r)$. 5. (##) A4T4 FINAL IMPLEMENTED A4T4a - Particle Update (`Particle::update`) `Particle::update` runs a sub-step collision loop until the remaining time `rem ≤ 1e-6` or a safety iteration cap is hit: - **No-velocity shortcut**: if speed is negligible, apply gravity and advance freely, then break. - **Ray cast**: build a ray from `position` along `velocity/|velocity|` with unlimited range; `d_contact > max_dist` filters out-of-timestep hits. - **No hit**: advance freely with a forward-Euler step, consume all remaining time. - **Approaching check**: if $-\hat{d}\cdot\hat{n} \le 0$ the particle is moving away from the surface; advance freely. - **Radius correction**: `d_contact = tr.distance − radius/approaching` gives the travel distance at which the sphere surface just touches the triangle. If negative, the particle is already interpenetrating — reflect immediately without moving. - **Contact beyond timestep**: no collision this sub-step; advance freely. - **Collision**: advance velocity by gravity for `t_travel`, then reflect $v' = v - 2(v \cdot \hat{n})\hat{n}$. Place particle at `tr.position + n·radius`. Subtract `t_travel` from remaining time and continue the loop. After the loop: `age -= dt`. Return `false` when `age ≤ EPS_F`.
